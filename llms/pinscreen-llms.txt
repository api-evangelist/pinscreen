# Pinscreen

> Pinscreen is an American AI and VFX production company (founded 2015 by Hao Li) that builds advanced generative AI for film production and interactive experiences — AI lip sync, face reenactment, face swaps, de-aging, voice cloning, and photoreal 3D avatar digitization. Its Avatar Neo product generates a photoreal, riggable 3D avatar from a single photo, offered to developers as the Avatar Neo SDK (Unreal and Unity plugins plus a deployable avatar-generation API). Headquartered in Los Angeles with a branch in Abu Dhabi (UAE).

## Products
- [Avatar Neo Creator App](https://www.pinscreen.com/avatar-neo-creator-app): Create a photoreal 3D avatar from a single photo; customize body shape and export the 3D model as GLB or FBX, including animation rigs.
- [Avatar Neo Developer SDK](https://www.pinscreen.com/avatar-neo-developer-sdk): Incorporate high-fidelity 3D avatar generation from photos into 3D apps, games, and AR/VR experiences. Choose a platform (Unreal or Unity) and download the corresponding plugin; the plugin communicates with Pinscreen's servers, and the API can be deployed anywhere.
- [VFX Services](https://www.pinscreen.com/vfx-services): AI VFX for photorealistic digital actors — face reenactment, AI lip sync, face swaps, de-aging, and voice cloning.
